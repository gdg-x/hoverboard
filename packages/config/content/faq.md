## General

### Dates and Location

DevFest Stockholm takes place on **February 27–28, 2025** at **Kista Entré & Konferens**, Knarrarnäsgatan 7, 164 40 Kista, Stockholm, Sweden.

### Stay Informed

To stay up to date on the latest information on sessions, speakers, and activities, be sure to visit the [DevFest Stockholm website](https://devfest-stockholm.com/), follow us on [X (@gdgsweden)](https://x.com/gdgsweden), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/gdg-sweden/), and RSVP on [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/gdg-stockholm-android/). Join the conversation using our official hashtag **#DevFestStockholm**.

### Content Formats

During the two conference days, attendees will be able to attend technical sessions, hands-on workshops, keynote talks, and connect with developers, Google Developer Experts, and partners.

### Livestream & Recordings

Keynotes and select sessions will be shared on the [GDG Sweden YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@GDGSweden). Follow the channel and our social media channels for updates.

## Registration

### Tickets

Tickets grant access to all conference sessions, workshops, coffee breaks, and lunch. Accommodation is not included in the ticket price. Please register and bring your digital or printed ticket to check-in.

### Registration Terms & Conditions

- Tickets for DevFest Stockholm are for the full conference.
- Tickets must be used by the original registrant or assigned participant.
- Attendees agree to adhere to the [Code of Conduct](/coc) at all times during the event.
- At check-in, please present your ticket and valid ID to receive your conference badge.
- Photographs and/or video taken at DevFest Stockholm by the organizers may include your image or likeness for promotional and community purposes.
- By registering, you agree that organizers may contact you regarding event information and logistical updates.
- If you have questions regarding your ticket or registration, please contact [info@devfest-stockholm.com](mailto:info@devfest-stockholm.com).

## Attendance Details

### Language

All presentations and workshops at DevFest Stockholm are conducted in English.

### Badges & Venue Access

- You must wear your conference badge visibly to access sessions, workshops, catering areas, and after-hours activities.
- Conference badges are personal and non-transferable once issued.

### Event Attire

DevFest is a relaxed community tech event. Casual and comfortable attire is encouraged.

### Accessibility

Kista Entré & Konferens is fully accessible with step-free access and elevators throughout the venue. If you require special accommodations or assistance, please reach out to any staff volunteer or contact [info@devfest-stockholm.com](mailto:info@devfest-stockholm.com).

### No Soliciting

No unsanctioned commercial solicitation or selling of items or services is permitted at DevFest Stockholm.
